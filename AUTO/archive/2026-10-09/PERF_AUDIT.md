# PERF_AUDIT — Round 3 (freeze hunt)

Read-only audit of branch `perf/freeze-hunt` @ `19a5942`, 2026-10-08. Six auditors (one per section), one skeptical verifier per section that re-read every cited line, then a completeness critic. Verdicts: **confirmed** (code, frequency and mechanism checked), **plausible** (real code, speculative impact), **missed** (added by the verifier). Impact is the verifier's rating for a Quest 3 hitch or a player-visible bug. Line numbers are for `19a5942`; re-check them before editing.

## Headline

- No single per-frame code path explains a repeated multi-hundred-ms stall. Steady per-frame managed allocation is small (GC-1, GM-1…5: roughly 10–20 KB/s), and incremental GC is already on.
- Event spikes worth removing: RunBot `Instantiate`/`Destroy` per spawn and kill (12 GameObjects, 27 components, 4 leaked material clones each; SP-1/SP-2/SP-4), and `BeamImpact` `Instantiate`/`Destroy` per hit (SP-3).
- Ruled out with evidence (critic): audio loading (no clips), TMP dynamic atlas (static, ASCII only), uGUI canvas rebuilds (none), texture streaming, realtime GI, analytics, runtime Shader.Find/Resources/File IO/GC.Collect.
- Not observable today: device-level stalls (tracking loss, overlays, thermal, compositor). Hence the logger and logcat capture (CR-2, CR-3) come first.
- Link evidence from this PC (2026-10-08 20:56–21:02): Link transport healthy (Glitches 0, transmission ~4 ms every 5 s), OpenXR session FOCUSED throughout. So the freeze Hyun saw over Link is most likely the editor not submitting frames in time, not the cable. Unproven until the logger and the Profiler capture a freeze.
